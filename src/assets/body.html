TRAINING WHEELS

Stuck? Every question has help buttons

  • 💡 Hint — what the question is really asking. Never spoils.
  • ⌁ Gists — each choice as one line of pseudo-code.
  • ◦ plain — a choice rephrased in simple words.
  • Reveal — the answer + why every other choice loses.
  • 🤖 Ask Claude — copies the question for a walkthrough in Claude.
  • 🔊 Listen — hear the question, choices, or full answer reasoning read aloud, with the words highlighted as it reads. Pick voice & speed under ⚙ Voice.

In Exam mode, Easy / Medium / Hard decides how many of these exist.
